## Decision-relevant findings

The current official Nota Informativa is deposited and effective **31 July 2026**, reached through the [official document index](https://www.cometafondo.it/documenti-del-fondo/statuto-e-nota-informativa/). URLs containing old upload years are not reliable version indicators: read the effective date inside each document. The index lists Statuto updated 29 July 2026. [Contribution sheet, pp.1–2](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-i-scheda-iii-i-destinatari-e-i-contributi/).

For this eligible new member, minimum employee contribution is **1.2% of contractual minimum**, enabling employer **2.2% of contractual minimum** because enrollment occurs after 5 February 2021 and before age 35; ordinary employer rate is 2%. All future TFR is contributed for a first occupation after 28 April 1993. Electing a higher employee percentage changes its base to remuneration useful for TFR. Do not multiply every percentage by RAL. [Current contribution sheet p.2, table and notes 1–2](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-i-scheda-iii-i-destinatari-e-i-contributi/).

**Under-35 is an acquisition condition, not an evidenced age-35 expiry.** The employer association specifies enrollment before the 35th birthday, contribution of the employee minimum, and preservation of the acquired right even on joining another employer applying this CCNL. It gives no age-35 termination. Model an acquired eligibility flag, while acknowledging that future contractual amendments or employment outside this CCNL can change future contributions. Do not claim an irrevocable lifetime rate. For old TFR-only silent members the relevant age is when they activate personal contributions. [Confindustria Toscana Centro e Costa, 23 May 2022, Requisiti/Eccezioni](https://confindustriatoscanacentroecosta.it/fondo-cometa-aumenta-il-contributo-aziendale-per-gli-under-35/). This predates July2026 automatic enrollment: do not apply its TFR-only description to new automatic members.

The sheet labels annual charges as estimates dependent on actual fund expenses, with surpluses/shortfalls allocated or carried forward.

| Operation | Published charge |
|---|---:|
| Transfer, redemption, advance | €10 each |
| Reallocation/switch | First free, subsequent €10 |
| Beneficiary designation | First free, subsequent change €5 |
| Pension benefit / RITA | No operation fee |
| Benefit with salary assignment/delegation | €20 |

Individual-prerogative expenses are **50% lower through reserved area with SPID/CIE**. Thus eligible €10 online operation becomes €5. Switching generally moves existing capital **and future contributions**, requires12 months between reallocations; initial explicit member enters Reddito and may change free after welcome letter without waiting12 months. [Investment sheet p.2](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-ii-scheda-i-le-opzioni-di-investimento/).

ISC is a standardized comparison at annual contribution€2,500 and assumed4% return, at2/5/10/35-year horizons; it is **not an additional debit**. Model actual fixed and asset charges; do not subtract ISC as well. Likewise historical net returns already embed asset expenses; avoid subtracting them twice. Direct member fees are excluded from quoted compartment returns. TER reports actual historical compartment expenses and has another denominator; do not substitute it mechanically for ISC or the tariff.

## Compartment policy, risk and history boundaries

Current policy references: [Investment sheet pp.5–9](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-ii-scheda-i-le-opzioni-di-investimento/) and [official financial-management page](https://www.cometafondo.it/gestione-finanziaria/).

| Compartment | Current policy / equity | Horizon; risk control | Guarantee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monetario Plus | Pure bonds/money market; short duration | Up to5yr; ex-ante volatility1.25% | None |
| Sicurezza | Primarily bonds; equity maximum30% | 5–10yr; volatility6% | Conditional capital protection |
| Reddito | Diversified balanced; equity maximum37% | 5–10yr; ex-ante volatility8% | None |
| Crescita | Balanced; benchmark55% equity/45% bonds; permitted equity45–65% | 10–15yr; TEV5% | None |

Volatility limits are management risk controls, not maximum losses. Crescita is not a100% equity fund. Its benchmark changed **3 August2026**, while Reddito added specialist benchmark mandates then (initially approximately88% total-return multiasset,9% bond specialist,3% equity specialist). Historical Reddito2025 narrative permits50% equities, which is not the current37% cap. Preserve policy vintages in backtests.

Sicurezza guarantee operates at **31 May2031** convention expiry and specified earlier pension, death, permanent disability reducing work capacity below one-third, unemployment exceeding48 months, or medical-advance events. Ordinary switch/transfer/other withdrawals are not listed guarantee triggers. Guaranteed value is starting net assets at convention inception plus subsequent net contributions, adjusted for withdrawals; it is not unconditional daily nonnegative performance. [Investment sheet p.6](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-ii-scheda-i-le-opzioni-di-investimento/).

From2026 TFR Silente is incorporated in Sicurezza; historical sections separately report TFR Silente and Sicurezza2020. At31 December2025 assets were Monetario€1,431,563,060; TFR Silente€482,765,898; Sicurezza2020€1,282,259,180; Reddito€11,225,711,772; Crescita€2,318,108,821. These are dated historical assets, not current assets. [Investment sheet pp.10,14,17,20,24](https://www.cometafondo.it/parte-ii-scheda-i-le-opzioni-di-investimento/).

Explicit members initially enter Reddito. New automatic members: age≤45 Crescita,46–59 Reddito,≥60 Monetario Plus; old tacit hires through30 June2026 use Sicurezza. Hiring/enrollment route and date matter; do not assume a25-year-old explicit member starts in Crescita. Automatic enrollment and general statutory transition: [legislation.md](legislation.md).

## Contract renewals and conflicts

The June2026 FIM table calls the agreement **2025–2028**, and current Cometa July2026 contribution sheet retains2%/2.2%. The [Federmeccanica2024 negotiating proposal](https://www.federmeccanica.it/area-stampa/comunicati-stampa/rinnovo-contrattuale-2024-2028-federmeccanica-e-assistal-propongono-il-primo-ccnl-esg.html) proposed2.2% for all and2.5% for new under35/women; a proposal is not an implemented rate. Do not import2.5% into the engine. Current evidence supports2.2% for this profile. The current note calls the older CCNL signature21/02/2021 while its qualifying enrollment date is5 February2021; preserve the operative date and flag the editorial discrepancy rather than changing it.

A [Confindustria Pesaro Urbino clarification15 October2026](https://www.confindustria.pu.it/ccnl-per-lindustria-metalmeccanica-e-della-installazione-di-impianti-previdenza-complementare-e-fondo-cometa/) says CCNL employer contribution is conditional on actual Cometa membership and minimum employee contribution and not portable to retail products. **Do not use that15 October statement to settle later statutory reform:** cross-check the later portability legislation/timing in [legislation.md](legislation.md). Model employer contribution separately by eligible destination and effective law, not as universal employer cash available for ETF investment.
